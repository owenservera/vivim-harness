A tour of the actual interface — not a mockup of an idea

Your AI accounts.
Your machine. Your world.

VIVIM opens a dedicated, signed-in browser for the ChatGPT or Claude account you already have, keeps everything you save on your own PC, and gives you one place to carry it forward — on your desktop, and reaching toward your phone. No API keys. No second account. Nothing is sent to an AI until you press Send.

Every screen below is redrawn using the real labels, colors and layout from VIVIM's own interface files. Where a feature is built in code but hasn't been confirmed on a real phone or with a live AI reply, it's marked built, not yet proven live instead of claimed as working.

01 — Starting VIVIM

Double-click VIVIM.cmd. That's the whole install step.

The launcher (scripts/windows/VIVIM.cmd → VIVIM.ps1) starts one local Studio server and opens one dedicated browser window — the only browser VIVIM controls. You sign in to ChatGPT or Claude yourself, the same way you always do. VIVIM never asks for a password and never calls a provider's API with a key.

Built and running on Windows
02 — Your World

One home for your AI conversations, projects and notes.

This is VIVIM's main screen — the same shell used for chatting, saving conversations, building projects and notes, and running your Personal Agent. The views across the top (Home, Build my VIVIM, Personal Agent, Capture, Compare, Saved conversations, Projects, Notes, Find, Transfer, Relations, Workspace, Composed workspace, Settings) are the real navigation labels in the product.

Built and running on Windows
03 — Build my VIVIM

Start from a template, or let your AI design the screens.

The Build my VIVIM view starts you on a small personal app — a notes list, a checklist, a dashboard — then lets your own signed-in ChatGPT or Claude suggest more screens. Nothing is added until you tap Add these screens; no code runs and nothing is installed on your machine.

Built and running on Windows
04 — Personal Agent

A durable identity that keeps your tasks and memory on your PC.

Your Personal Agent tracks Work — tasks, next steps and progress — across sessions, and keeps notes in three tiers: Immediate, CANON and Curated. Nothing is imported from your provider conversations automatically, and nothing is sent to an AI without your approval.

Built and running on Windows
05 — Your phone

Ask my AI and Build your app, from your phone, over your own WiFi.

The phone surface connects to your PC, not to the internet directly: your phone opens a page that talks to the same Studio that's already running at home, which talks to your already-signed-in browser. Ask my AI continues a real chat with ChatGPT or Claude; My apps opens the small apps you built. There is also a native Android shell that wraps this same page for pairing and offline recovery.

Built, not yet proven live on a real phone

What's actually confirmed today

From the project's own Android build log, dated 2026-10-29:

  • The Android shell compiles, packages and signs into vivim.apk (signature verified with apksigner).
  • It has not been installed on a device, opened, paired, or used to send or receive a real message.
  • Phone pairing, reconnect-after-Wi-Fi-change, and the root Back menu are described in code and docs, but unobserved on real hardware.

Why this page says "not yet proven live"

VIVIM's own standard is: a build or a passing check is not proof of real behavior on a real device with a real AI reply. Until someone installs the app, pairs a phone, and watches a live answer arrive, this surface stays labeled honestly rather than claimed as finished.

06 — Compare

Ask the same question. See both answers side by side.

The Compare view sends your question through your own ChatGPT and Claude accounts, one at a time, each starting a fresh conversation. Nothing is combined or summarized for you — you read both real answers yourself.
